# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, validated against KSA build version 2026.11.10.5801: a modular lunar lander kit of 18 parts with 38 variants. Hex Deck and Oct Deck; Wall, Half and Taper panels in isogrid, skin, radiator, gold foil and silver foil looks; Solar Wall and Solar Half panels; Thrust Cone, Core Column and Deck Strut; Sphere, Capsule and Drum tanks in bare, gold blanket and silver blanket looks, which hold the true volume of their shape; the engines Mizar and Mizar Fixed; a short and a long landing leg that deploy; and a Lattice Ramp that folds and deploys.
